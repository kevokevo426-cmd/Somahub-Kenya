Social Project

Natasha Precious - Grade 7 Pink | 178 words

The task seemed difficult until we decided to work together. It was a challenge at first because we kept on bickering and fighting so that each of us could say what we were thinking. I was trying to calm the group members down because we had less than twenty minutes to finish our project. We were making a windsock. Once they started calming down, we divided ourselves into three groups. One was in charge of cutting the thread, another handled the making of the sock itself, and the last one read the instructions on how to make the windsock. We cooperated very wonderfully. Once the three groups finished, we came together and connected the windsock. It was not as good as we had hoped, but it was better. We were competing with other groups which made a rain gauge, an anemometer and other instruments for measuring weather. When we were all done, we went and sat down on the chairs in front of the table. Our subject teacher came and judged us, and our group became second. Even if we didn't come first, we still had a chance to cooperate as a team.

🔑 LITERARY KEY 1: Personification

• Wind as a person: "the wind whispers through the sock" - wind given human action (whispering)

• Animals Discussed: Teamwork like ants / bees - "We worked like busy bees building our hive"

• Imagery used: Visual - colorful windsock dancing, Auditory - bickering voices calming, Tactile - cutting thread
